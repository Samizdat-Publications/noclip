# Layout inspiration from Stewart (S02, 2026-10-01)

Stewart shared a photo of a hand-drawn Backrooms floor plan (promotional material from the 2026 film). Per the IP rule in CLAUDE.md we do NOT copy its layout, labels or landmarks. We take only general design language, which matches real office plans and Backrooms folklore:

- Wall outlines are rarely clean rectangles: stepped, jagged runs with small jogs and notched corners; some 45 degree chamfers at corridor bends.
- Big halls carry grids of columns, including cross (plus) shaped columns, not only square ones.
- Free-standing T and L shaped partition walls and short wall fins inside rooms and along corridors.
- Dense clusters of small rooms next to wide open halls; long corridors that wrap around the outside of a block (perimeter loops) and dogleg.
- Thick solid wall masses (service cores), dead ends, narrow passages, ramps and stairs that lead somewhere else, zones left deliberately undefined.

How it feeds the generator (Level0Gen): wall jogs and fins, plus-shaped columns in pillar halls, T/L partitions in big rooms, perimeter corridors, service-core blocks, dead-end stubs. Logged as DECISIONS D016.
