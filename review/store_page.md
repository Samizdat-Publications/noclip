# NOCLIP

You were closing up the furniture store when you found the wall in the back, outlined in duct tape. You walked through it.

Now there is carpet that never dries, a hum that never stops, and fluorescent light falling on rooms nobody built. You still have your camcorder. It sees things you can't.

Something lives in the halls, far too big for them. It hears you run.

You will die down here, often. A company in white suits keeps a post just inside, and they pay for your tapes. Spend it on a flashbulb, road flares, quieter shoes. Find the stairwell in each place and go deeper, where it is darker and stranger.

First-person surreal horror roguelite for one player.
