# Player Review

## Gut reaction
I'd call it a very good-looking Backrooms hallway with a real hook, and it isn't a finished horror game yet. The camcorder-sees-what-you-can't idea works. Doors show up as glitching white outlines, and a tripod camera casts a giant shadow of a tall figure on the wall (frame_12). Those are the shots I'd screenshot. Between them, I get a lot of identical yellow fog and a lot of near-black screens where nothing is happening. The camcorder in my hands looks like a placeholder next to everything else. I'd keep playing to see where the figure thing goes, but nothing has scared me yet.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] Some frames are almost pure black. In frame_08 and frame_09 about 90 percent of the screen is nothing, and frame_05 is mostly black. If a player stares at that for any length of time, it reads as a broken game. I wanted dark rooms that are still readable, with a faint shape or an edge catching light, so the fear comes from what I can almost see.
2. [hurts] The first-person camcorder model looks unfinished (frame_14, frame_16). It's flat matte black with no specular, no wear, no label and no screen. It's a big shape in the corner of frame_14. Everything else in the scene has grime and detail, and this is the thing in my hands the whole game. It should look like worn plastic and metal.
3. [hurts] Every space is the same yellow-brown. The wallpaper, carpet, fog and lights all land in one narrow warm band across frame_01 through frame_18. Fifteen minutes in, I'd stop noticing where I am. I wanted at least one area that breaks the palette, like a cold dead-tube blue or a bad green, so the yellow means something when it returns.
4. [hurts] The big open rooms (frame_16, frame_17) are empty and murky. The carpet turns into blurry sandy mush under the fog, and the far walls disappear. Nothing in the room makes me want to walk toward it. A ladder is barely visible at the back of frame_17. I wanted objects and silhouettes at different distances to pull me through.
5. [hurts] The monster is under-sold. In sheet_04 it's a tiny black figure standing in fog, and in sheet_03 it looks like it hovers. It reads as a placeholder man. Only the shadow in frame_12 and the glitch-outline doors actually felt threatening. I wanted that same menace in the figure itself, with a wrong silhouette, a wrong height or wrong motion.
6. [nitpick] Some surfaces still look unfinished. There is blocky stair-stepped shading on the right wall of frame_01. Frame_11 has a big blank white foreground wall and a flat brown slab for a ceiling, and the pillar there looks like three stacked boxes. These pull me out of the hallway look that the rest of the game sells.
7. [nitpick] The "carpet that never dries" pitch isn't visible. The floors in frame_02 and frame_17 show dark stains but no sheen or reflection of the ceiling lights. Wet carpet catching the tubes would add a lot.

## Working, don't touch

- Doors that glitch into white noisy outlines only on camera (frame_03, frame_15). This is the best idea in the build.
- The tripod camera with the tall figure's shadow, tape label and red stencil (frame_12). It tells a story with no words.
- The chalk "LOOK THROUGH IT" tutorial drawing in the hallway (frame_01). It teaches the mechanic without a UI prompt.
- The deep ceiling grid with its dark and backlit gaps, and the soft bloom under the light panels (frame_18).
- The chevron wallpaper and the door and trim detail. The tall twisted room in frame_04.
- Performance. It's smooth and steady with big headroom.

## Compared to last build
Better.
- **Shadows and depth:** the ceiling now has a real tile grid with gaps and a missing tile, where the old ceiling was flat (prev_03). The tripod room now has the figure shadow and props (a crate, a tape), where prev_05 was a flat orange wall.
- **Contrast:** the old frames were washed out and hazy (prev_04). This build has darker rooms and a stronger light-versus-dark split.
- **Trim, doors and clutter:** the baseboards and door frames are cleaner and there is more clutter (frame_11, frame_01).
- **Glitches:** the camera glitch is lighter and more controlled than the heavy smear in prev_02.
- **Not fixed:** the palette is still one note, and the big halls are still murky.

## Steam review one-liner
Great camcorder hook and a few shots I'd frame, but too much same-yellow fog and black screen so far. Wishlisted, not sold yet.

## What I want next
1. A scare payoff that matches the camcorder idea, where the thing you only see through the lens gets closer, bigger or wrong, and I need to look at it anyway.
2. A visibly different place or lighting mood (cold, green, wet and reflective) to break up the yellow. Along with it, a finished camcorder model in my hands that looks as worn as the rest of the world.
