# Player Review

I could only judge the stills and contact sheets. I can't play video, so I did not watch review/inbox/video/clip.mp4.

## Gut reaction
This is better than "yellow rooms with a camcorder filter", and the new pillar hall in fog is the first thing here I'd screenshot. The VHS death replay and the "END OF TAPE / ON THE SHELF 4/14" card are a real hook. They make dying feel like part of the game instead of a punishment. But the monster is a tiny glitchy smudge in the distance, and the camcorder in my hands looks like a pistol. The pillar hall is also the same frame repeated over and over, and the office areas are still flat. Promising, not there yet.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The camcorder in my hands looks like a handgun. In frame_05 and sheet_01 (row 2) it is a black pistol grip, a gloved hand and a small screen full of static. I thought "shooter". Give me a bulky camcorder with a strap and a viewfinder, held with two hands.
2. [hurts] The monster is unreadable. In frame_15 and frame_18 it is a flickering vertical smudge. The paper-strip, long-forearm design from the changelog is nowhere visible. I wanted one clear frame where I go "oh no", even if it's only on tape.
3. [hurts] The pillar hall repeats itself. Frames 06 to 09, 12, 13 and 17, and every cell of sheet_02 and sheet_04, are the same fog, the same pillars, the same ceiling glow. It's pretty at first and then monotonous. Change the pillar spacing, break the line with a collapsed section, a different light colour or a wall, and give me one wrong object to aim for besides the chair and ladder.
4. [hurts] The office areas are flat. In frame_03 and frame_05 the walls are even yellow, the partitions are plain green slabs, and there is no light spill, no desk clutter and no shadow play. Compare frame_04, where one dark hall with a lit end is far more interesting. I wanted the whole office dimmer, with real pools.
5. [hurts] The pillars look like plastic. Same notched top, same blue-white, almost no wear or stains, and they barely meet the floor (frame_13, frame_17). Dirt, wall-colour change at the base, or cracks would help a lot.
6. [nitpick] Floating props. The ladder in frame_14 and the chair in sheet_02 look pasted on, with no contact shadow. The chalk door in frame_11 is cleaner than before but also lost its water stain.
7. [nitpick] Hitching. A 59.7 ms worst frame and 5 frames over 25 ms at an otherwise smooth 93 fps will show up exactly when I turn to look at something.

## Working, don't touch
- The VHS look: REC, timestamp, tracking tears, the battery icon and the playback and rewind overlays (frame_01, frame_18, sheet_03).
- The "END OF TAPE" card with its location name and shelf count (frame_20). Keep it dry and deadpan.
- The tripod shadow of a tall figure on the wall (frame_02).
- The warm exit door and red sign at the far end of the fog (frame_14, frame_16). That is the best colour and focal moment in the build.
- The fog and bloom on ceiling panels in the pillar hall (frame_08, frame_13).
- The dark corridor with a lit far end and a ladder and chair as silhouettes (frame_04). Pools of light work.
- The footprints in the carpet (frame_10).
- The chalk door with "HERE" (frame_11), and the tape revealing it as a hole (frame_21).
- The store-page pitch: "you will hear it before you see it, and you will only ever see it on tape".

## Compared to last build
Better. The old stills are a clean yellow corridor with an even ceiling (prev_01, prev_02), the tripod against a flat wall (prev_03), a plain room with a water cooler (prev_05) and the chalk door with a dresser (prev_06). The new build has real contrast: dark halls with a single lit end (frame_04), and a whole new fog-and-pillar space. The tape itself now has a replay, a rewind, a death card and a visible monster. Where it hasn't moved: the yellow office is still the same flat look, and the chalk door lost its water stain (prev_06 against frame_11).

## Steam review one-liner
"The tape-replay-as-death idea is a great hook and one room looks gorgeous, but I'm still waiting to be scared by something I can actually see. Wishlist for now."

## What I want next
1. One clear, genuinely wrong silhouette for the monster, readable on tape in under a second. Right now it's noise.
2. Make the other rooms as interesting as the pillar hall. Dim the offices, break the repetition in the hall, and give the camcorder in my hands a camcorder's weight and shape.
