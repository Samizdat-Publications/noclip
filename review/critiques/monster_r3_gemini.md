## Best and worst shot
**Best:** Option 5. The distance and fog hide the terrible textures. The silhouette is actually unsettling when you can't see the details.
**Worst:** Option 3. Close-up without the VHS filter exposes every flaw. It looks like a shiny, weightless action figure hovering in a hallway.

## What is wrong with it (ranked, max 5)
1. **Texture (Options 1, 3, 4, 6):** It looks covered in baby oil or wet plastic. I want matte, sickly, dead pale skin.
2. **Weight (Options 1, 2, 3):** It floats. A giant in a small space needs to sag, drag, and press heavily into the carpet. 
3. **Face (Options 3, 6):** "Calm and eyes closed" currently just looks unfinished and blank. It needs subtle, unsettling human anatomy to work.
4. **Lighting (Option 3, 6):** The glossiness catches every light in the room. Turn down the specular reflection completely.
5. **The Grab (Option 6):** The hands look stiff and posed like a toy. Needs to feel heavy and desperate.

## Keep
The silhouette in the fog (Option 5). The VHS filter (Options 2, 4), which is mandatory to hide how bad the base model looks. The scale of it barely fitting in the hallway.
