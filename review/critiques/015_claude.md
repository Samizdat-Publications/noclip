# Player Review

## Gut reaction
This is the yellow-office-carpet liminal look done cleanly, but it isn't a screenshot-worthy game yet. The hallway in frame_07 looks good and the tripod room in frame_11 is the best image in the set. Most other frames are yellow wallpaper, grid ceilings and fog. It's a very tidy Backrooms. I'm not scared and I'm not wowed. The camera-glitch door in frame_04 is the first thing that felt like this game's own idea, and I want more of that. I'd keep playing a little to find out what the tripod and the shadow are about.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The held camcorder in sheet_01 (top right panel) is a mess. It fills a third of the screen as a lumpy black blob with two dented sphere shapes that look like a bad scan. It looks like a different game. I want a small, clean camcorder that reads as an object. Compare the neat one in prev_06.
2. [hurts] The environment is too samey. Frames 03, 06, 07, 16 and 19 are all the same yellow wallpaper, carpet and tile grid. After a few minutes I stop reading space and lose all sense of place. I want rooms with a different identity, like the chair room in frame_20, which is the only place that felt new.
3. [hurts] The huge-room frames (frame_09, frame_18) are the weakest looking. The giant walls are flat, blurry wallpaper with no detail or wear, and the thin white line (a trim or rail?) floats on the wall in frame_15 and frame_16. They read as a scale bug instead of something unsettling. Add visible ceiling or floor context so the scale feels intended.
4. [hurts] Dread is missing in the lit spaces. Nothing in frames 02, 03 or 07 suggests anything is wrong. The best dread frames are the dark ones (frame_17, frame_19) and the tripod room (frame_11). I want more of the "something was here" feeling and less empty hallway.
5. [hurts] There is no monster on screen. The wall shadow in frame_11 is a great reveal, but nothing in this set pays it off. A single glimpse at the end of a hall would sell the whole build.
6. [nitpick] Frame_01 is a blank wall of noisy beige. The first thing a player sees looks like a loading error. Start on the hallway with the chalk camcorder sign.
7. [nitpick] Doors and trim (frame_10, frame_17) are plain grey boxes with no hinges, handle or wear. The partition panels in frame_15 are black slabs. These props are the weakest surfaces next to the wallpaper.

## Working, don't touch
- The wallpaper close-up (frame_12). The chevron pattern, relief and stains are the best material in the game.
- The camcorder overlay: REC dot, timestamp, battery bars, grain, vignette and scanline tear (frames 01, 05, 20).
- The chalk door and "HERE" arrow turning into a glitching portal with the tripod behind it (frame_04, sheet_01). That's the most original beat.
- The tripod room (frame_11): warm key light, a tall shadow on the wall, a stenciled red marking and a crate. It is lit like a real scene.
- The dark rooms with a bright doorway (frame_17, frame_19, sheet_04). The contrast and the lone chair feel properly eerie.
- Ceiling tiles with water stains and a visible grid depth (frame_03, frame_16).
- Performance. Keep it where it is.

## Compared to last build
Better in a few places, worse in one.
- Better: the hallway now has a clean wallpaper print. The old one in prev_01 had a smeared look and a heavy water-stain streak. The dark rooms are readable now. prev_03 was a nearly black frame with a blown-out door, while frame_17 and frame_20 show shape and furniture. The fog is lighter. The old foggy hall in prev_06 was a sea of haze, and frame_07 keeps depth with less murk.
- Same: the ceiling grid, the carpet and the yellow palette.
- Worse: the held camcorder. In prev_06 it was a small readable prop. In sheet_01 it is a huge black lump with sphere-like bulges.
- Different, not better: the old impossible-room shot (prev_02, with a floating door and a hanging ceiling slab) was weirder than anything in the new wide-room frames.

## Steam review one-liner
"Gorgeous wallpaper, great camcorder glitch door, and a monster I only saw as a shadow. The Backrooms are well lit now, but I want to be scared."

## What I want next
1. Show me the creature once, even if it is far away or half-seen through the camcorder. Pay off the shadow in the tripod room.
2. Break up the yellow. Give me three or four rooms with a clear identity, like the chair room, the tripod room and the cubicle rows, so I can tell where I am. Use the glitch-portal trick in more places.
