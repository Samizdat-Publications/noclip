# Player Review

*(The handback tool was blocked by a hook, so the report is here as plain text.)*

## Gut reaction
It looks like a competent Backrooms-style game, and I couldn't find the hook that sets it apart. The wallpaper has real character: a chevron print with grime and stains, and it holds up close in frame_10 and frame_11. The camcorder overlay, the fog and the ceiling tiles all work. But in 21 stills and 4 sheets, nothing is scary and nothing is truly impossible, and no monster appears anywhere. It plays as a pretty yellow-office walking sim. The best moments are the CRT-glitched doorway (frame_04, frame_06) and the "WE ARE THE COPY" door-in-a-door (frame_08). Those two are the only things I'd screenshot.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] Nothing I can see is threatening. Across every frame and sheet there is no creature, silhouette, or "something moved" beat. The store page promises slow dread and rare scares, but I only see rooms. I wanted one unmistakable wrongness: a figure at the end of the corridor in frame_05 or frame_19, or a room that is different when I look back.
2. [hurts] The first-person camcorder prop looks unfinished. In frame_19 the camera is a chunky black box with flat grey faces and a hard-edged lens barrel. It sits in the lower right of the frame and looks like a placeholder next to the wallpaper. It's also the thing I stare at all game. I wanted visible wear, a strap, a scuffed body, and a lens that catches the fluorescent light.
3. [hurts] Doors and trims look like plastic cutouts. The white door frames in frame_08, frame_17 and frame_21 are flat colour with no wear, no paint chips and no thickness. Against the detailed wallpaper this reads as cheap.
4. [hurts] Several rooms are very flat. frame_15 is an orange-brown room with two grey doorways, almost no light variation and no features. frame_21 is similar. Both could be any room in the game, and I'd lose my place. They need a focal prop, a stain, a light with a story, or something to look at.
5. [hurts] The tripod room is a bad first beat. frame_03 and frame_07 show a nearly monochrome orange room with a camera on a tripod, and sheet_01 shows I'm in it for several seconds. The lighting is a single flat orange wash. The red stencil ("G1 S-14") on the left wall is the only interesting detail and it's cut off at the frame edge. I wanted a stronger first screenshot.
6. [nitpick] The glitch overlay is overdone in places. frame_04 is nearly unreadable, and frame_06 outlines the doorway in white noise. It's fun once. If it fires often it would look like a video artifact instead of a haunting.
7. [nitpick] frame_13 (11:03:55) is so foggy and washed out that the room loses all structure. The fog hides depth cues I'd like to see. frame_14 has a hard black ceiling cut-off at the top, so the upper third just stops.

## Working, don't touch
- The new chevron wallpaper (frame_10, frame_11, frame_17). It reads as a real printed paper and carries the whole look.
- The fog haze under the ceiling panels in long corridors (frame_05, frame_18).
- The camcorder HUD: REC dot, battery bars, timestamp, scanlines and vignette (frame_01, frame_09).
- The "WE ARE THE COPY" recursion door (frame_08). It's the best idea in the build.
- Performance. It's rock solid.
- The footprint trail on the carpet (frame_20) and the chalk door outline with "HERE" (frame_12). These are the right kind of small story detail.

## Compared to last build
Better, but not by a huge margin.
- The wallpaper is a clear upgrade. The old build (prev_01 to prev_06) had a flat, nearly featureless wall with faint vertical lines. The new one has a visible chevron pattern, stains and speckle.
- The corridors now have dark gaps and varied ceiling heights (frame_12, frame_14, frame_20) instead of the uniform tunnel in prev_01 to prev_06. That's real progress on mood.
- The warm light on the walls is more contained. The old build had a green-tinted wash (prev_03, prev_05) and a red emergency light at the far end (prev_05). Those are gone or reduced.
- Still the same: the corridor-and-doorway shot, the flat door frames, and the lack of any threat.

## Steam review one-liner
Gorgeous wallpaper, great camcorder filter, runs like butter, and I have still not been scared once.

## What I want next
1. Show me something that is wrong. One figure, one door that wasn't there, one room that changed. Put it where the camera is already looking, in a long corridor like frame_05, and make me decide whether I saw it.
2. Give the objects the same love as the walls. A scuffed, believable camcorder in my hands, doors with paint and wear, and one memorable prop per room, so frame_15 and frame_21 stop looking like empty boxes.
