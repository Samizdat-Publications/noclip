# Player Review

I looked at all 21 frames, 4 sheets and the 6 previous stills. I did not open `video/clip.mp4`, and I can't hear audio from stills.

## Gut reaction
It's the best-looking Backrooms-style corridor I've seen from a small team, but it doesn't scare me yet. The VHS portal glitch (frame_03, frame_04) is the one image I'd screenshot. The pillar hall (frame_18, frame_20) is a close second. The held camcorder (frame_16) looks like a grey prop from a student project and makes the rest look cheaper. Too many frames are either flat, evenly lit yellow (frame_02, frame_17) or so dark I'm staring at a black wall (frame_08, sheet_02). I'd keep walking to see where the loops go, but nothing here has made me anxious yet.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The first-person camcorder prop is ugly. In frame_16 it's a huge, smooth grey lump with a donut lens, lit flat and taking up a third of the screen. Next to the believable walls and ceiling it breaks immersion every time I raise it. I wanted a worn, black, plasticky handheld with a visible viewfinder, strap and scuffs, smaller in frame. In frame_06 and sheet_02 it's a black blob, which is better because you can't see the model.
2. [hurts] Rooms are either blown flat or too dark. Frame_02 and frame_17 are evenly lit mustard with no falloff, so the room reads as a render test rather than a place. Frame_08, frame_11, frame_12 and all of sheet_02 are so dark the key prop, the chalk door, is barely readable. I wanted the chalk door lit by one distant tube so I can read it and also feel what's behind me.
3. [hurts] Not much is happening in the beats I can see. Sheet_02 and sheet_01 are long static stares at a wall. Frame_10 and frame_12 are empty doorways with nothing at the end. For "slow dread" I need small wrongness in frame: a chair that has moved, something at the edge of a lit room, a door that was closed.
4. [hurts] The pillar hall is a bit too clean and low-poly. In frame_18 and frame_20 the pillars are faceted yellow boxes with the same wear, and the right-edge pillar has a smeared stepped edge. The fog and floor light patches carry it, but I wanted dirt, cracks, tape marks or one pillar that's wrong.
5. [nitpick] Wallpaper and carpet tiling is noticeable in long shots. The vertical stripe pattern in frame_07 and frame_17 repeats evenly, and the carpet in frame_02 looks like a flat noise texture. Wet stains (frame_01, frame_10) help, but the "carpet that never dries" promise isn't visible yet. I wanted a sheen or sodden patches that catch the tube light.
6. [nitpick] The orange emergency light slivers on the ceiling (frame_18, frame_19) look like stuck-on rectangles. Nothing around them takes the orange glow. I wanted the wall or tile around them to pick up the colour.
7. [nitpick] Wall geometry looks bent near the frame edges in frame_02 and frame_16. The left wall curves like a lens bulge, and the first looks wrong rather than uncanny. I'd lessen it or make it look deliberate.

## Working, don't touch
- The VHS look: REC dot, battery bars, timestamp, tracking line, scanline softness and bloom (frame_01, frame_03, frame_13). It sells the 1996 camcorder fantasy.
- The portal and glitch effect around the taped door (frame_03, frame_04, sheet_04). It feels like the camera is seeing something it shouldn't.
- The soft floor light patches and volumetric fog in the pillar hall (frame_18, frame_19, sheet_03).
- The green-tube room (frame_15), a good cold break from the yellow.
- The chalk door "HERE?" prop (frame_11) and the survey-crew stencil "HD G1" (frame_09), which tell a story without text dumps.
- The small props: the work light, milk crate and tripod camcorder (frame_09) feel lived in.
- Fog fading the long corridors (frame_07).

## Compared to last build
Better, mostly in lighting and mood.
- The previous hallways (prev_02, prev_05, prev_06) were bright, flat and evenly lit, almost like a render. The current build has real contrast and dark pockets (frame_10, frame_14), so rooms feel like places.
- New this time: the green room (frame_15), the orange emergency lights (frame_18), the pillar hall, and the chalk door and survey room set pieces. All are improvements.
- Lost: the figure from prev_01 is gone from every frame I have. I liked the silhouette (despite how flat it looked in prev_01), so I hope the monster isn't gone altogether.
- Still the same: the ceiling-tile floor-light object in prev_04 is gone, which is good. Flat, mustard-lit frames (frame_02, frame_17) look like prev_05 and prev_06, so that problem isn't solved.

## Steam review one-liner
Gorgeous tape glitch and a hall of pillars I wanted to get lost in, but I'm still waiting for the part where something looks back.

## What I want next
- Make the camcorder in my hands look as good as the world, and let me use it. Film something, see the screen change, and see the VHS glitch react when I point it at the wrong thing. The portal in frame_04 shows how good that can look.
- Give every dark room one readable, wrong detail in a lit spot, and put the threat at the edge of the light. I want to see something move in the fog at the end of the pillar hall before it stops being empty.
