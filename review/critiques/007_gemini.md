# Player Review

## Gut reaction
It's yet another Backrooms walking simulator. You wander endless yellow halls, pull out a VHS camcorder sometimes, and look at chairs facing walls. The persistent elements like the chalk marks are a neat idea, but visually, it's completely flat and lacks the sterile, oppressive atmosphere the concept needs. Worse, it runs terribly for a game that looks like it came out fifteen years ago. Unless the camcorder mechanic gets incredibly wild later on, I've already played this game twenty times before.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] **Performance.** My RTX 5070 Ti is chugging at 56 FPS and dropping to 33 FPS for empty hallways with basic geometry. This needs serious optimization before anyone will play it.
2. [hurts] **Missing shadows.** Look at Still 1 and Still 3. The fluorescent lights create bright spots on the floor, but the pillars don't block the light at all. Everything looks like it's glowing uniformly.
3. [hurts] **No depth or occlusion.** In Still 2, where the walls meet the ceiling and corners, there is absolutely no shading. The rooms feel paper-thin and floaty because the geometry doesn't ground itself with shadows.
4. [hurts] **Generic asset feel.** The wallpaper, the ceiling tiles, the door at 00:54.500—it all looks completely stock. There's nothing visually separating this from a weekend game jam project.
5. [nitpick] **The carpet isn't wet.** The store page promises "carpet that never dries," but the floor just looks like flat, dry carpet with dark brown splotches painted on it. It needs some sheen or specularity to actually look damp.

## Working, don't touch
- The persistent environmental storytelling is great. Seeing the chalk 'X' (00:38.500) and the tally marks (01:00.500) grounds the player and makes the loop feel meaningful.
- The sudden, aggressive glitch transitions into the VHS camcorder mode (00:10.500, 00:31.500) are sharp and disruptive in a good way.

## Steam review one-liner
Another trip to the Backrooms, but this time it brings your framerate down with it.

## What I want next
Fix the lighting so the pillars and walls actually cast shadows, and give me a puzzle or a hidden door that I can *only* see when looking through the camcorder.
