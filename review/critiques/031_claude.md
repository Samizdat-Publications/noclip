# Player Review

## Gut reaction
It's a good-looking Backrooms corridor with a convincing 1996 camcorder skin, and I'd say it stays at "solid but familiar". The tape layer does most of the work. The raw 3D underneath is flat, evenly lit and repetitive. The monster is a black cutout standing at the end of a hall (frame_08), so I never felt it was a threat. The red EXIT stairwell (frame_16, sheet_03) is the one image I'd screenshot. The new blue "Cold Rooms" depth reads as a recolor of the yellow one, not a new place. I can't judge audio or the actual chase from stills, and there is a clip.mp4 I can't watch.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The monster is a black silhouette with no detail (frame_06, frame_08, sheet_04). It never gets close or lit in anything I was given, so it has no scary shape or texture. I wanted at least one frame where it is lit, a wet sheen on skin, a face, a limb that moves wrong.
2. [hurts] Depth 2 "The Cold Rooms" looks like depth 1 with a blue filter (frame_14, frame_18 vs frame_09, frame_13). The same wallpaper, ceiling tiles, corridor width and door frames appear. Going deeper should look and feel stranger, as the store page promises. I wanted a different room shape, a different material, a different kind of light.
3. [hurts] The lighting is flat and uniform in the larger rooms. Frame_15 is one yellow wash. Frame_03 and frame_07 are blown-out ceilings over crushed floors. The lights bloom but don't light anything (frame_12, frame_13). I wanted bright pools with dark between them, and shadows from the furniture and pillars.
4. [hurts] The untaped views are crushed. In frame_05 about a third of the picture is pure black, and the black pillar in frame_03 is a slab with no detail. It looks like unlit geometry, not dread.
5. [hurts] The corridor repeats. Frames 09, 12, 13, 14 and 18 are the same hall with only the color changing. The brightly lit doorway-lined hall is the whole game so far, and it's nice for ten seconds.
6. [nitpick] The EXIT sign is mirrored ("TIXE") in the untaped view (frame_11), while the taped view reads correctly (frame_16). It looks like a bug.
7. [nitpick] The portal outline in frame_01 is a noisy white sparkle. It reads as a compression artifact more than a doorway to somewhere else. The tripod behind it is a good detail, though.

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder layer: REC dot, battery bars, timestamp, scanline tearing, static on death, and the lean and warp in motion (frame_08, frame_16, sheet_02).
- The red EXIT stairwell. The red light against the yellow hall is the best color moment in the build (frame_16, sheet_03).
- The relief on the chevron wallpaper (frame_10) and the water-stained ceiling tiles (frame_04, frame_06).
- The tripod and its stretched shadow on the wall (sheet_01).
- The "DEPTH 2 THE COLD ROOMS" black card with the stacked v marks (frame_17-style card). It sets the descent idea well.
- The cold blue palette itself (frame_13, frame_18) is a good direction. It just needs different rooms.

## Compared to last build
- Better: the taped image is brighter and easier to read than prev_01 and prev_02, which were murky and heavily vignetted. The wallpaper detail is stronger (frame_10 vs prev_01).
- Better: there is a whole new depth with its own palette (frame_13, frame_14, frame_18).
- Same: the monster is still a dark silhouette in a corridor (frame_08 vs prev_01, prev_04). I see no change in its surface.
- Worse or lost: the dark red stairwell glare in prev_04 to prev_06 (the glow bleeding into black corridor, a real mood piece) isn't shown in this build's raw frames. The taped version in frame_16 is a tighter, flatter shot.

## Steam review one-liner
Great camcorder skin over a very familiar yellow office, and I still haven't seen the monster properly. Wishlisted, not recommending yet.

## What I want next
- Show me the monster lit, close, and wrong. I want a surface, an animation, and one frame where I back away from my screen.
- Make depth 2 a different place. New wall material, new ceiling, and lights that fail or pool, so going down feels like going somewhere stranger and not just colder.
