# Player Review

## Gut reaction
This is a good-looking Backrooms corridor, and I mean that in a narrow way. The hallway shots (frame_06, frame_08) would get screenshotted. The camcorder glitch frames and the dark-room-with-lit-doorway shots (frame_11, frame_07) are the real hook. But I've seen yellow wallpaper, drop ceilings and damp carpet a hundred times. I'd want one frame I haven't seen before, and the one monster beat I got is a small gray figure that looks pasted into the room. It runs smoothly and the camera tricks are cool, but right now it reads as a polished backrooms demo, not its own game.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The monster doesn't scare. In sheet_01 (frames 1 and 9) it's a small gray figure that looks pasted into the doorway and then the room. It doesn't pick up the room's light, it has no real silhouette, and it's far too small. I wanted something that lingers in the dark edge of a room like frame_11, not a mannequin standing in a lit room.
2. [hurts] The far distance falls apart in the big fogged rooms. In frame_17 and frame_18 the far walls look like floating panels with gaps between them and an obvious flat fog line. The carpet turns into rippled sand dunes. Those two shots look unfinished next to the corridors.
3. [hurts] The bright, evenly lit rooms (frame_01, frame_03, frame_17, frame_18) go flat and tinted. Everything is the same mustard yellow with no pools and no real dark corners, which is where the dread should come from. The dark versions (frame_11, frame_19) are much better, so the contrast between the two is too big.
4. [hurts] The camcorder item looks like a gray handgun (frame_10, sheet_01). I can't tell it's a camera. It also has no hand or arm attached, so it floats.
5. [nitpick] The mop bucket in frame_08 is a flat glowing yellow blob that ignores the lighting. It looks like a sticker down the hall, and the same thing shows up in frame_09.
6. [nitpick] There are visible texture seams in the wallpaper. Look at the left wall in frame_13, where there are horizontal bands of brightness, and the carpet seam lines in prev_05 are only partly gone.
7. [nitpick] Too much sameness. Every room is the same wallpaper, ceiling and carpet palette, and only the red exit-sign and stenciled paint (frame_09, frame_19) break it up. I wanted at least one wrong object or color.

## Compared to last build
Slightly better. The previous frames (prev_01 to prev_06) had a murkier, greener cast, and the carpet showed diagonal seam lines (prev_05, prev_06). The current carpet is smoother and warmer, the ceiling lights glow more with haze around them (frame_08, frame_13), and the corridor has more depth. The tripod-in-doorway beat (prev_06) now has a figure shadow too. But the fog in the big rooms is thicker and exposes the floating far-wall panels, and the carpet now looks like sand there. Net: better in corridors and lighting, worse in the big open rooms.

## Steam review one-liner
"Beautiful corridors and a camcorder that tears like real tape, but the monster is a mannequin and every room is the same yellow."

## What I want next
1. A monster that lives in the dark parts of the rooms, with a real silhouette that catches the light and is only visible through the camera (the tripod shadow in frame_20 is the right instinct).
2. One room that breaks the yellow rules: wrong scale, wrong color, or something impossible, so the surreal space gets a payoff beyond a big empty hall.
