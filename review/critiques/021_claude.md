The mustard wallpaper hallway is clean, saturated and readable, and I'd screenshot the tripod room (frame_12), the lone chair in the grey pillar hall (frame_16) and the amber-lit far door (frame_18, frame_19). It feels more like a lovely render of a creepy place than a creepy place. The gun-shaped thing in my hand (frame_02, frame_03) bugs me, and the pillars are plain white boxes. The camcorder overlay and static hits are the best part of the image. The monster is barely there.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The thing in my hand looks like a pistol (frame_02, frame_03, sheet_02 cell 5). A black chunky barrel with a red lens, bottom-right, reads as a sidearm. The store page promises a camcorder. In a game where I'm meant to be helpless, a gun silhouette undercuts everything. I wanted a recognisable handheld camcorder with a flip screen or viewfinder.
2. [hurts] Some darkness looks like broken lighting, not mood. In frame_06 the top quarter is a flat black slab with a hard straight edge, and the left wall is a pure black void. In the pillar hall the ceiling ends in a hard floating shelf (frame_15 across the top, frame_17 right side). I wanted dark that falls off gradually and still shows some surface.
3. [hurts] The pillars are plain, untextured white boxes (frame_14 to frame_17). The notched cap is the only detail. Nothing is stained, scuffed or painted, so they look like a showroom. The cold blue-grey is a good colour contrast, but the hall is too clean. I wanted grime, water lines, scuffs and warm bounce light on them.
4. [hurts] The main hallway is too bright and cheerful for dread (frame_11, frame_02, sheet_01). The walls glow evenly, almost every tile reads lit, and shadows are soft. It's a pretty yellow corridor. I wanted a few dead fixtures, one flickering panel, and more pools of dark between lit stretches.
5. [hurts] The monster doesn't register. In frame_01 and the sheet_04 sequence it is a tiny dark flat shape in the fog that stays in the same pose. It's the right idea, but I felt nothing. I wanted a change in posture or distance between frames, a head turn, or something wrong with its proportions.
6. [nitpick] A faint rectangular decal edge is visible on the wall behind the tripod (frame_12, around the camera head). The taped note on the camera is illegible. Props like the crate and VHS tape look pasted on and only half grounded.
7. [nitpick] The two static blackouts (frame_09, frame_19) show nearly the same dark blob on the right. The first one is great. By the second it looks like a template. The wrong exit sign in frame_08 is also a smeared, unreadable "EXIT" through the camera, so the joke doesn't land.

## Compared to last build
Mixed.
- Better: the yellow hall materials. It's more saturated and the wallpaper pattern and ceiling tiles read much better than the muddy beige in prev_01. The far door is now actually lit with an amber glow (frame_18, frame_19) instead of a black rectangle (prev_05). The wall light pools are nicer.
- Worse: the pillar hall's mood. Last time it was brown and heavily fogged, with bare bright fixtures glowing in murk (prev_03, prev_04), and it felt oppressive. Now it's cooler, cleaner, flatter and evenly lit (frame_14 to frame_17).
- Worse: the new unlit ceiling-slab and hard-edge artefacts (frame_06, frame_15).
- Same: the camera overlay, static hits and the tripod.
- Gone: the ladder from prev_06, and the standing figure only appears in the camcorder view now (frame_01).
