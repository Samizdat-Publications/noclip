# Player Review

I only have stills and sheets, so everything below is from those. The clip.mp4 in the inbox is a video I can't view. I have no audio at all.

## Gut reaction
This is a very convincing yellow-carpet, drop-ceiling liminal office, and it's moodier than I expected. The camcorder view with the "REC / PM 11:24 OCT. 14 1996" overlay and the glitchy chalk-door portal (frame_04, frame_13) is the one thing here that looks like it's only in this game. Off the camcorder, though, it's a lot of the same hallway, and the same wallpaper in the same mustard. The "monster" is a tiny pale grey silhouette at the end of a corridor (frame_12) and I wouldn't be scared of it. It looks good in a screenshot and I'm not yet scared or surprised. I'd play another hour to see if the portal rooms pay off.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The hand-held camcorder prop in frame_08 reads as a pistol. It's a grey, noisy, grimy tube pointing out of the lower right of the screen with a dark muzzle-like hole. Next to the clean, soft world it looks pasted on and wrong. In sheet_03 it also shows up as a black blob. I wanted something clearly consumer-camcorder shaped, lit by the room, that sits in the same world as the walls.
2. [hurts] The monster doesn't scare me. In frame_12 and sheet_04 it's a small, pale, semi-transparent figure standing still at the end of a bright corridor, and it looks like a placeholder. I wanted it sharper, closer to the dark, or doing something wrong between frames. The huge smear on the wall in frame_05 is far scarier and should be the template.
3. [hurts] Sameness. Frame_15, frame_16, frame_17 and sheet_02 are all the same mustard wallpaper, carpet and ceiling grid in the same hue. Only the red exit glow in frame_20 and the tripod room's warm orange break it. I wanted a second colour temperature, a ruined or different room type, or something out of place in the hallways.
4. [hurts] Performance hitches. A 27 fps 1% low and a 65 ms spike at 1440p on a laptop 5070 Ti will feel stuttery when you turn into a bright, fogged room, and stutter breaks tension.
5. [nitpick] The wavy, soft gradient along the bottom of walls (frame_08, frame_09, sheet_02, frame_06) looks like a shadow or occlusion artifact, not dirt. It has a wobbly edge that reads as a smear. I wanted either a clean contact shadow or clear damp-stain grime.
6. [nitpick] The ceiling light panels in frame_01 and frame_15 are blown-out white rectangles with almost no glow bleeding into the surrounding tiles. Fluorescent tubes should buzz and throw haze. Frame_03 and frame_20 do this better.
7. [nitpick] The wallpaper tiling is visible as horizontal bands on the left wall in frame_18, and the carpet is a plain noise texture with no wear paths, stains or footprints, even though the store page promises "carpet that never dries".

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder overlay and VHS treatment (frame_04, frame_06, frame_10, frame_12): pillarbox, REC dot, battery, timestamp, tracking tears, and the vignette.
- The chalk door glitching into a portal that shows the tripod room (frame_04, frame_13). It's the best image in the build.
- The long fogged hallway with ceiling lights fading into haze (frame_11, frame_16, frame_17).
- The dim office floor with cubicle partitions and a chair silhouette, with lit rooms glowing in the distance (frame_19).
- The tripod room: a warm orange pool of light in an otherwise dark room, with a red stencil, a crate and a crooked tape label (frame_03).
- The red exit glow bleeding onto the ceiling (frame_20), and the door opening onto the fog and pillars.
- Wallpaper detail up close (frame_17).

## Compared to last build
Better overall. The old stills (prev_01 to prev_06) are brighter, flatter and more yellow-saturated. Rooms were evenly lit with little contrast. The ceilings were almost blown out in prev_01 and prev_03. The new frames have real dark zones, light spilling through doorways, fog, and furniture (cubicle partitions, a chair, a desk in frame_19). The chalk portal was already in prev_06 but now looks sharper in frame_13. The red exit-light glow is in both builds. Still the same: the carpet is plain, the colour is nearly one hue, and the camcorder overlay is unchanged.

## Steam review one-liner
"The VHS camcorder and the chalk-door portal are great, and the fog hallways look like a nightmare version of my old office. But the ghost is a grey smudge and the yellow rooms need more variety before I'm scared."

## What I want next
1. A monster that you can see only through the camcorder, or that fills the frame once, with the same level of detail and menace as the wall smear in frame_05.
2. More rooms like the pillar hall behind the door in frame_20 and the dark cubicle floor in frame_19. They need different colours, scales and lighting, so each portal or seam is a surprise and not another yellow hallway.
