# Player Review

## Gut reaction
This nails the Backrooms audio perfectly, but visually it feels like a dozen other basic indie clones I've seen on itch.io. The core mechanic of using the camcorder to see hidden things is a fantastic hook, but the environment lighting is so flat it ruins the illusion of a real physical space. Right now, it feels like a strong mechanical prototype that desperately needs an art director to come in and do a major lighting and texture pass.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The lighting is completely flat. In the pillar room (see the video at 01:08 or the stills), the pillars cast zero shadows on the floor despite the bright ceiling lights right next to them. It makes the entire room look like a 2004 game relying on ambient light and fog. I need deep, directional shadows to ground the space.
2. [hurts] The light fixtures themselves are unconvincing. They are just bright white rectangles slapped on the ceiling. The acoustic tiles immediately next to them are completely dark. There needs to be light bleed, bounce, or a slight emissive glow affecting the immediate geometry.
3. [hurts] The monster is a placeholder. Seeing a static, featureless black silhouette standing perfectly rigid in the distance isn't scary; it just looks like an unfinished default rig. It needs a subtle idle animation—a twitch, a breathing heave, or a slight head tilt—to feel alive.
4. [hurts] The materials lack physical properties. The yellow wallpaper and wet carpet desperately need normal maps and proper specularity. The pitch says the carpet "never dries," but it looks like matte construction paper. I want to see a slight, sickly sheen on the floor when the light hits it.
5. [nitpick] The camcorder transition is jarring. Snapping instantly from holding the camera in the bottom right corner to a full-screen VHS overlay breaks the immersion. I want an animation of bringing the camera up to my eye, or better yet, let me look at the actual flip-out screen in the 3D space.

## Working, don't touch
- The audio design is phenomenal. The droning, slightly pulsating fluorescent hum and the squishy, wet footsteps match the store page pitch perfectly and do all the heavy lifting for the atmosphere.
- The core mechanic of the camera revealing things the naked eye can't see is brilliant and executed clearly right from the start.

## Steam review one-liner
Incredible, oppressive sound design trapped inside a visually flat Backrooms clone.

## What I want next
A complete lighting overhaul with proper cast shadows, and for the monster to actually move or twitch when I look at it through the lens.
