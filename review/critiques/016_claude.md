# Player Review

## Gut reaction
Okay, this is the best-looking yellow-office-liminal thing I've seen in a while. Wallpaper, carpet and stained ceiling tiles all look lived-in, and the hazy fluorescent hallways (frame_04, frame_17) are properly screenshot material. The camcorder overlay with its REC light, timestamp and glitching is a strong hook. It's still one colour in one repeating kit, though. The thing in my hand looks like a gravel-textured brick, and the monster is a tiny black cutout that only shows up through the camera. I'd keep playing to see what the doors lead to, but I don't feel afraid yet. I can't judge sound at all because I only have stills.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The camcorder viewmodel looks bad. In frame_19 and frame_18 (and sheet_01/sheet_03) it's a big gravel-textured block with a flat purple dead lens and a bright cartoon-white switch. It sits in the lower right and doesn't look like the glossy plastic gear you see on the tripod in frame_08. I wanted a believable handheld camcorder, smaller and smoother, with a lens that catches light.
2. [hurts] The monster is a black paper-doll. In frame_03, frame_20 and sheet_04 it has no depth, no surface and no light on it. It appears to stand slightly above the carpet, and the carpet reflection in sheet_01 and sheet_03 reads like water. It also just stands still. I wanted something that feels like it occupies the space: a wrong silhouette, a change in pose between glances, a shadow on the floor.
3. [hurts] The palette and kit are monotone. Nearly every frame is the same mustard wallpaper, the same ceiling grid and the same carpet. By frame_16 and frame_17 I've seen the whole look. I wanted a colour shift, a different material, a dead-end that looks different, or one room that breaks the rule.
4. [hurts] Some frames are dark to the point of being unreadable. Frame_02 is a nearly black room with a faint chalk door. Frame_12 is a murk with one wall spot. Frame_10 is a black void with a lit strip. The dark rooms have almost no secondary light, so they look unlit rather than scary. I wanted warm bounce light, a visible edge, or a light source that explains the gloom.
5. [nitpick] The carpet in the big hall (frame_13, frame_18) is a huge flat plane with soft blotches and no wear paths, stains, footprints or seams. Next to the walls it looks like a fog-covered ground texture. The store page promises footprints and chalk marks, and I can't see any footprints.
6. [nitpick] The fog is milky and uniform (frame_13, frame_16, frame_17). It hides draw distance, but it also flattens everything past 20 m into one beige wash. Shafts of light or pockets of denser haze would be better.
7. [nitpick] The ceiling light panels are bright rectangles with soft bloom. In frame_17 and frame_18 they glow, but the tiles directly around them barely brighten. Light doesn't pick out the grid edges the way real fluorescent light would.

## Working, don't touch
- The hallway look: wallpaper chevron detail, baseboards, stained tiles, door frames (frame_04, frame_17).
- The camcorder overlay, tracking glitch and noise breakup, especially the door dissolving in frame_05 and the transitions in sheet_01 and sheet_03.
- The amber tripod room and the figure's shadow on the wall (frame_08). Best-lit image in the set.
- The walk-through-the-door reveal into a huge pillared hall (sheet_02, frame_18), and the ladder in the background that gives it scale.
- The oversized wall with the doorway at its foot (frame_12). That's the right kind of wrongness.
- Chalk door outline with an arrow and "HERE" (frame_16, prev_06). Good storytelling.
- Performance.

## Compared to last build
Better, in several places:
- The corridor in frame_17 now has glowing panels and clear wall falloff. The previous hallway (prev_03) was flatter, with a dimmer carpet.
- Doors and rooms are better framed. Frame_16 and sheet_02 have more depth than the empty two-door room in prev_04.
- The big hall with pillars and fog (frame_18) is new and a big step up.
- The chalk door in frame_16 is clearer than the glitched version in prev_02.
- The tripod room with the strong spot and long shadow in frame_08 is new.
- The camera feel is similar to before, with a wider range of glitches. prev_01 and prev_05 are the blank wall and a flat wall close-up. The new glitches are more varied.
- The camcorder viewmodel is the same or arguably worse. The worn texture exposes how rough the model looks up close.

## Steam review one-liner
Gorgeous hallways and a convincing found-footage camera, but I'm still waiting for something to actually scare me.

## What I want next
1. A first real "wrong space" moment that isn't just a bigger room. Show me a hallway that clearly changes length when I look away, filmed so the tape catches it.
2. Make the monster feel physically present. It should have a surface, cast a shadow, and be closer or in a different pose every time the camera finds it. Then fix the camcorder in my hands so it matches the quality of the world.
