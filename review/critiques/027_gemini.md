# Player Review

## Gut reaction
I am so burnt out on Backrooms games, and this starts looking exactly like the 500 others on itch.io. But the camcorder mechanic—only seeing the monster through the lens—is a genuinely terrifying hook that actually got my heart rate up. The audio is doing heavy lifting here, because visually, especially in the second area, it looks like an unfinished blockout. It has a great core gimmick, but it needs a serious lighting pass to not look like a cheap asset flip.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The lighting in the grey pillar room (Image 3, video 0:38 onwards) is completely flat. It looks like unbaked, ambient white light with zero shadows or contrast. It destroys any sense of scale or dread.
2. [hurts] Performance is inexplicably terrible. You have a game that looks like a 2012 indie title, yet it's dropping to 37 FPS on a high-end laptop GPU and stuttering hard (96ms frame time spikes). 
3. [hurts] The base environments are too generic. The yellow wallpaper and grey pillars have been done to death. Give me a reason to care about *this* specific impossible space.
4. [nitpick] The monster's base model (Image 1) is just a standard "tall dark stringy guy." The glitch effect makes it scary, but the design itself is a tired trope.
5. [nitpick] The footstep audio sounds disconnected from the floor. It feels like a generic "carpet_step.wav" playing at a fixed interval rather than matching the physical weight of the player's movement.

## Working, don't touch
- The core mechanic of the monster only appearing through the camcorder is fantastic.
- The visual and auditory violence of the VHS static when looking at the entity.
- The hidden neon "seams" (Image 2) visible only on tape—this is exactly what the game needs more of.

## Steam review one-liner
A genuinely terrifying camcorder gimmick trapped inside yet another flatly-lit Backrooms clone.

## What I want next
A complete lighting and shadow overhaul for the grey pillar room, and more environmental puzzles relying on the hidden doors/seams seen in the camcorder.
