# Player Review

## Gut reaction
The pillar hall is the first time this game has looked like something people would screenshot. It's cold, foggy and grey-blue, with a red exit sign glowing at the far end (frame_13, frame_14). The yellow corridors are still the same corridor I've seen in a hundred Backrooms clones. The monster is a black glitch silhouette that works on tape (frame_16, frame_21), but in the hallway it looks like a cutout standing still (frame_01, sheet_04). I'd keep playing for the tape-versus-eye idea and the tripod room, and I'd be waiting for the yellow half to get a personality.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The yellow corridors are generic Backrooms (frame_03, frame_15, sheet_01). Same wallpaper, same ceiling, same hum-yellow, with nothing off in them. I wanted one thing per hallway that's wrong, like a stain pattern, a light out of rhythm, or a wall angle that's a degree off.
2. [hurts] The hallway monster doesn't threaten me (frame_01, sheet_04). It stands far away, barely changes between frames, and the white stripes on it look like a texture error. It reads as a person-shaped cutout, not something made of paper. I wanted it to be wrong in how it moves or what it does.
3. [hurts] The pillar tops have ugly notches and flaps where pillar meets ceiling (frame_08, frame_11, frame_13). In the best-looking room, this looks like shadow or geometry errors. It also shows in the tape view (frame_16).
4. [hurts] The pillar room has almost no detail to read (frame_09, frame_12). The pillars are identical and the carpet is flat grey noise. The mop bucket (frame_12) and ladder (frame_13) are the only landmarks, and they're lonely. I wanted stains, a pillar with something wrong on it, and a clutter cluster.
5. [hurts] The doorway into the white void is blown out (frame_05, frame_06). The room beyond is almost pure white fog, with no contrast and no sense of what the player is looking into. It's meant to be a wow moment and ends up flat.
6. [nitpick] The held ladder looks huge and clips into the view (frame_14), covering a third of the screen right next to the nicest vista in the game.
7. [nitpick] The near-ceiling light panels look like stickers (frame_15, frame_03). The hot rectangle has a soft halo, but the tiles around it barely brighten. The tape view (frame_17) is much better at selling light bleed.

## Working, don't touch
- The cold grey-blue palette and fog in the pillar hall, with the warm exit-sign glow at the far end (frame_13, frame_14).
- The camcorder look: REC dot, battery icon, date stamp, tracking tears, the static burst and the PLAY death replay (frame_20, frame_21).
- The tape-only hole in reality, with the figure's shadow on the wall in frame_10 and the door that looks different on tape in frame_07. That is the game.
- The black glitch silhouette on tape (frame_16, frame_21).
- The tripod room with the torn VHS tape, the red stencil and the tally marks (frame_10). It's the only place with a story.
- The doorway with the white void behind it, set into a plain wall (frame_06).

## Compared to last build
Better, and clearly so in one place. The pillar hall used to be a warm beige blur (prev_06). It's now cold, deeper and more contrasty, with proper light pools and fog layers (frame_09, frame_13), and that's the biggest improvement. The open yellow office rooms (prev_03, prev_04) are replaced in the current stills by a darker, more varied storeroom (frame_04, frame_06), and the corridor is slightly less flat (frame_15 against prev_02). The monster silhouette is unchanged in kind. The yellow corridor is the same as before.

## Steam review one-liner
Spooky camcorder Backrooms with one gorgeous cold pillar hall; make the rest of the place half as good and it's a keeper.

## What I want next
- Make the yellow stretches as moody as the pillar hall: darker, patchier lighting, one visible wrong detail per hallway, and fog or dust to build depth.
- Make the paper thing frightening at a distance with your own eyes. Give it stranger movement and a clear silhouette, and use the tape for the part you can't see.
