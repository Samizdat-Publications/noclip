# Player Review

## Gut reaction
It looks like a proper Backrooms game, and the camcorder is the best idea in it. The tripod room (frame_17) and the VHS glitch on the glowing door (frame_16, sheet_03) are the two moments I'd screenshot. Everything else is the same yellow corridor over and over. The fixtures are lit but the rooms mostly aren't, so it reads like a nice level-0 mod and not yet a game with its own look. I'd keep playing to see what the camcorder shows me. I wouldn't be excited by the base image.

The inbox also had video/clip.mp4. I can't play video from here, so this review covers only the stills, sheets, perf.json and the store page.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] Everything is the same mustard yellow, and the lit hallways are evenly bright (frame_06, 08, 15). Nothing in the palette or the light levels says "something is wrong here". I wanted one corridor lit sickly, one almost black, and a real contrast between them.
2. [hurts] The light doesn't convince. Fixtures glow, but the wall colour flips between green and orange across a single wall (frame_10, frame_04), and the carpet has hard diagonal seam lines (frame_10, frame_12). It reads as a texture or tint problem, not light bouncing around a room. I wanted the pool under a panel to be obviously bright and the space between panels to fall off.
3. [hurts] The monster is a standing silhouette seen only in the camcorder, and it does nothing in the frames I got (sheet_02, sheet_04). The glitch dissolve is a nice touch, but I need to feel it getting closer or turning toward me. Right now it's a prop.
4. [hurts] The tall room (frame_19) is the only spatial surprise, and its vertical light streaks look like a stretched texture, not a light shaft. The floating door is a good idea, but it needs more going on around it. I want more of these moments.
5. [hurts] The long-distance haze is much thinner than in the previous build (see Compared). Distant rooms are now crisp (frame_10, frame_12, frame_13). That kills the sense of depth I liked before.
6. [nitpick] Props and partitions are blank. The grey divider in frame_14 is a flat slab with no detail, and the chalk door drawing on the wall behind it is nearly unreadable (frame_14, frame_21). The chalk is the "place remembers you" hook, and I can barely see it.
7. [nitpick] Wallpaper shows vertical streaking and moire at mid distance (frame_09, frame_13). At 1440p the stripe pattern looks like noise on far walls.

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder look: REC dot, four-bar battery, timestamp, and tracking glitches (frame_02, frame_11, frame_16, frame_20).
- The glowing door with the VHS tear around it (frame_16, sheet_03). It is the best "impossible" image in the build.
- The tripod room (frame_17). The warm wall spill, the red stencil "G1 S-14", the dropped tape and the heater all add to it.
- Ceiling tile grime and stains (frame_03, frame_18). They make the tiles look old, not clean.
- Wallpaper emboss up close. The red exit marker at the end of the hall (frame_15) is a good small detail for the depth.
- Rectangular light pools along the panels (frame_01).

## Compared to last build
Mixed, about the same overall.
- Better: the lit panels now cast a rectangular pool (frame_01 against prev_03), and corner darkening and grime in the room corners are stronger (frame_04, frame_12). The carpet has more patchy variation than the repeating weave in prev_02.
- Worse: the soft distance haze in prev_02 and prev_06 is mostly gone (frame_10 and frame_12 have no haze). The floor also lost its glossy damp sheen (prev_06 against frame_09). The wet-looking stain line on the wall in prev_02 is gone too, which was a good "carpet that never dries" cue that supported the store page.
- The dark-hallway mood of prev_04 is not repeated in the new frames. The nearest match is frame_18.

## Steam review one-liner
"The camcorder is a great idea and the tripod room is gorgeous, but I'm still walking the same yellow hallway. Wishlisted, not yet sold."

## What I want next
1. Put the haze and the damp sheen back, and use real contrast between rooms. Make some rooms oppressively dark and make a few light fixtures flicker or sag, so the warm pools feel like something I'm afraid to leave.
2. Make the thing in the corridor commit. Let it move closer between camcorder looks, and let the glitch reveal something I'd rather not have seen.
