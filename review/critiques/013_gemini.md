# Player Review

## Gut reaction
This absolutely nails the Backrooms vibe, but it's the camcorder mechanic that actually elevates it from a generic walking sim into something I want to play. Looking through the viewfinder to reveal a hidden room behind a chalk outline is a killer hook and looks incredibly smooth in motion. The ambient sound design is oppressive in the best way possible. The bright rooms look a little flat and dated right now, but the core loop of hunting for seams in reality is instantly engaging.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] Missing shadows and ambient occlusion. Look at the second image: the tripod is inches from the wall but casts almost no shadow, making it look pasted into the scene. The corners of the rooms also lack depth.
2. [nitpick] Flat materials. The ceiling grid in the fourth image and the yellow wallpaper everywhere look like plain, flat image textures. They desperately need normal maps so the fluorescent light catches the bumps, peeling edges, or physical grid lines.
3. [nitpick] Footstep audio doesn't match the pitch. The store page says "carpet that never dries," but the footsteps in the video sound like heavy boots on dry, standard office carpet. Add a slight, gross squelch to sell the dampness.

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder reveal mechanic. The visual transition from solid wall to hidden room when raising the camera is seamless and highly satisfying.
- The ambient room hum. It is perfectly tuned to be annoying and anxiety-inducing without blowing out my speakers.
- The UI and tape UI elements. The minimalist battery/REC indicators feel authentic.
