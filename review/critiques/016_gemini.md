# Player Review

## Gut reaction
I've played a dozen Backrooms games this year, and at first glance, this looks like another one. The yellow wallpaper and moist carpet are all here, but the lighting is incredibly flat, making it look like a first-time Unity project. However, the mechanic of only seeing the monster through the camcorder is genuinely tense. It turns a generic walking simulator into something that actually makes me panic when I have to put the camera down.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The lighting is completely flat. Hallways look almost fullbright (frame 1), and there's zero ambient occlusion in the corners to give the space depth or mood.
2. [hurts] The carpet texture tiling is glaringly obvious, especially in the large room (frame 3). It breaks the immersion immediately.
3. [hurts] The background fluorescent hum is mixed way too high and never changes. It needs to be lowered and varied before players just mute the game.
4. [hurts] The monster design is boring. A dark, blurry humanoid (0:07 in video) doesn't live up to the really cool mechanic of only seeing it through the lens.
5. [nitpick] The texture on the camcorder itself (frame 4) looks like a procedural noise placeholder, not real worn plastic or metal.
6. [nitpick] In the large room (frame 3), the light hitting the floor is clearly from a round spotlight, but the ceiling fixtures are large square panels.

## Working, don't touch
- The core mechanic of the monster being invisible to the naked eye but visible through the camcorder is fantastic.
- The VHS visual effects and the escalating audio/visual glitches when the monster gets closer.
- The smooth, snappy transition between normal view and looking through the viewfinder.

## Steam review one-liner
A generic Backrooms walking sim saved by a genuinely terrifying camcorder mechanic, if you can get past the flat lighting.

## What I want next
More surreal, impossible geometry rather than just square office rooms, and a monster that looks truly bizarre when I finally spot it on tape.
