## The one that hunts me in the first yellow rooms
1. **Option 6:** It perfectly weaponizes the mundane environment of the backrooms, making me distrust the very lights I rely on to see.
2. **Option 8:** The fluorescent tube embedded in its back is a sick detail that ties it directly to the hum of the rooms, and ceiling-droppers always induce panic.
3. **Option 3:** Starting the game with these uncanny, fading corporate drones sets a deeply unsettling psychological tone before the heavy hitters show up.

## Deeper down
**Option 7** belongs deep down. When the player is already exhausted and the hallways start feeling tighter and more oppressive, turning a corner to find a giant, pale freak blocking the entire path forward will cause pure, unadulterated panic. **Option 5** also works further down where the office starts decaying and melting—it feels like the rotting soul of the damp carpet finally standing up.

## Make them scarier
*   **Option 6:** Do not give it footsteps. It should sound like grinding glass and buzzing electricity, and the lights in the room should violently flicker and pop right before it detaches from the ceiling to chase you.
*   **Option 8:** Turn off the ceiling lights in its patrol area. The only light source should be the flickering fluorescent tube embedded in its spine, casting horrible, swinging shadows as it crawls upside down toward you.
*   **Option 3:** Make them completely silent and completely rigid. They shouldn't have an animation cycle. They just appear closer every time you look away or blink, completely blocking your path with their dead, generic stares.

## Laugh or refund
**Option 1** and **Option 9** would make me refund the game. Option 1 looks like a Minecraft mob made of insulation foam bursting through the wall to offer me a refreshing beverage. Option 9 is the default "spooky shadow guy with claws" that every 14-year-old puts in their first Unity project. **Option 4** is also dangerously close to a quit moment; I am entirely sick of tall, faceless, long-armed humanoids walking slowly down hallways. Do better.
