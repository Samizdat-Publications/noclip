# Player Review

## Gut reaction
The camcorder stuff is the real hook. Seeing the chalk "HERE" door turn into an actual glowing doorway through the viewfinder (frame_15), and finding a tripod casting a huge shadow on the wall that looks like a person (frame_11), both gave me chills. Outside those moments I spent most of my time in the same yellow hallway, and this build lights it brighter and flatter than the last one, so the dread leaks out. The figure shows up and reads well as a silhouette, but it stays a small mannequin at the end of the hall and never gets close enough to scare me. Worth following, but right now it's a great VHS filter on a fairly generic Backrooms walk.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The hallways are lit flat and too bright. Frames 03, 08, 10 and 21 are all evenly lit pale yellow-green from end to end, and the walls get the same light at every point along their length. Last build (prev_03, prev_05) had deep dark gaps between lights and a dark near wall. I want the dead tubes and pools of real black back, so the next lit patch feels like somewhere I have to reach.
2. [hurts] The held camcorder looks like a placeholder. In sheet_03 (middle row, left) it fills a third of the screen mid-raise and is built from shiny black spheres and boxes, like an untextured toy. In sheet_01 and the last cell of sheet_03 it pokes into frame as a small black block. It's the main tool and the face of the game. I want a scuffed, real-looking 90s camcorder, or the raise kept out of shot.
3. [hurts] Too much of the same corridor. Frames 03, 04, 05, 08, 10, 16 and 21 are the same hallway with the same ceiling and the same carpet. Between the good set pieces nothing changes. I want more variety in what's along the way: furniture-store leftovers, dead-end alcoves, a ceiling dropped low, a hall that's too wide.
4. [hurts] The figure never gets close enough to feel. In sheet_04 it stands at the far end of the hall for about 3 seconds and barely moves. In frame_18 it's a fuzzy grey person in the middle of an open room. Two full-screen static frames (frame_06, frame_19) look like they're its moment, but all I get is noise. I want one shot where it's close, lit, and wrong.
5. [nitpick] Lighting glitches. Frame_17 has a blotchy, splattered patch of light on the far wall that looks like a smudge on the lens, not a light. In frame_18 and frame_20 the distant rows of ceiling lights break up into a glittery dotted band that looks like a bug, not part of the place.
6. [nitpick] The floating door in the tall room (frame_12) looks like a sticker. It sits flat, slightly crooked, with nothing leading to it. The thin vertical things on the left and right walls have colour fringing that makes them look broken. The idea is great, but it needs stairs or a ledge, or something under it to make it believable.
7. [nitpick] The full-screen static (frame_06, frame_19) wipes the screen completely. Twice in one short session is already a lot. If it hits on every figure sighting it'll turn into "screen goes grey" and stop being a scare.

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder reveal on the chalk door (frame_14, then frame_15). It's the best idea in the game and it looks great.
- The tripod rooms: a warm single light, a long shadow on the wall, and the spray-painted survey marks (frame_11). Seeing a tripod through a glowing doorway across a dark room (frame_18, frame_20) is also great.
- The VHS overlay: the timestamp, battery cells, blinking REC, slight scanline smear, and 4:3 framing (frame_01, frame_16).
- The surface detail: water lines at the bottom of the walls (frame_08), stained ceiling tiles (frame_21), footprints in the carpet (frame_09, frame_13), tally marks on the wall (sheet_02).
- The fog swallowing the end of each hallway.
- Performance. Keep it this smooth.

## Compared to last build
- **Worse: the hallway lighting.** prev_03 and prev_05 were darker, with a near wall falling into shadow and real gaps between lights. Frames 03, 08, 10 and 21 are brighter, flatter and paler green, and they lost that contrast and the dread that came with it.
- **Better: there are actual set pieces now.** The tripod room (frame_11), the chalk door turning into a seam (frame_15), the tall room with a door high on the wall (frame_12), and tripods filmed through glowing doorways (frame_18, frame_20) are all new and are the best parts of the build.
- **Same: the figure.** It's still a distant dark silhouette standing in the hall (prev_02 vs sheet_04).
- **Same: the big pillared open room.** prev_06 had the chalk door and fog, and this build's open-room shots (sheet_03) look about the same, with distant lights shimmering more.

## What I want next
1. Bring the darkness back to the hallways and make the stretch between set pieces less samey, so walking feels tense.
2. One up-close moment with the figure on tape, plus a camcorder model that looks like a real object in my hands.
