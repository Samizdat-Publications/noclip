# Player Review

## Gut reaction
It looks good in a Backrooms way, but not special yet. The pillar hall with the fog and the tiny red exit sign at the far end (frame_17, frame_19) is the one image I'd screenshot. Everything else is one mustard colour, so ten minutes in I'd stop being able to tell rooms apart. The camcorder footage is the best idea here: the REC overlay, the timestamp, the scratchy chalk door in frame_16. The monster is a dark standing silhouette I can barely see, and nothing in the stills shows it doing anything. It runs great and reads clearly. It still feels like a nice mood demo and not a game that wants to scare me.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The whole game is one mustard yellow. The corridors (frame_05, frame_07), the rooms (frame_03, frame_04) and even the pillar hall (frame_12, frame_15) are all the same hue and brightness. Nothing is dark, nothing is a different colour, and no area has its own identity. I wanted some rooms to go cold, dim or sickly, so I could tell where I am and so a change would feel threatening.
2. [hurts] The monster doesn't register. It is a tiny matte silhouette in the distance (frame_01) and I can't tell what it's doing from the sheets (sheet_04). I wanted to see it closer, see it shift between frames, and feel hunted.
3. [hurts] The rooms are lit evenly with no light-and-dark contrast. The big halls (frame_03, frame_14) are bright and hazy everywhere. Real dread needs dark stretches, a dead tube, a corner I don't want to look into. Frame_19 is the nearest it gets, and that is the one room that's properly dark. I wanted more of that.
4. [hurts] Nothing moves. Across sheet_01 and sheet_03 there is no flicker, no light going out and no movement in the distance. The only life is the camera shake and glitch. Even small flickers or a far light dying would help a lot.
5. [nitpick] The pillars are plain boxes with the same notch on every one (frame_12, frame_13, frame_15). Their bases have blocky dark-diamond shadow patches that look like rendering mistakes and not grime. I wanted dirt at the base, some damage, and a few different pillars.
6. [nitpick] The chair in frame_17 hovers, with its feet visibly above the carpet and a smeared shadow that doesn't match. It is a lovely single prop, so the mistake stands out.
7. [nitpick] The missing or tan ceiling tiles (frame_09, frame_10) are bright flat colour rectangles. Behind the dropped tiles I wanted dark gaps with pipes or darkness, not flat orange cards.

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder look: the 4:3 frame, REC and battery icons, timestamp, tracking tears and static burst (frame_01, frame_10, frame_16).
- The chalk drawings: the door with the "HERE" arrow, the "LOOK THROUGH IT" camera, and the red stencil with its arrow in frame_19. They teach the player without a tutorial and make the place feel lived in.
- The fog and haze in the pillar halls, with the far red exit glow (frame_17, frame_19). It is the best depth in the build.
- The tripod camera in the small dark room (frame_19). It is lit from one side, the room is a dark box, and I wanted to know who left it there.
- The long corridor with the ceiling lights thinning into the distance (frame_05, frame_07).
- Frame rate and smoothness (perf.json).

## Compared to last build
Mixed, and I'd call it slightly better.
- Better: the halls are lighter and the ceiling no longer looks black and heavy. The corridors have more light at the far end and a visible grid of lights. The wall grime is subtler.
- Better: the camera overlay reads more cleanly, and the camcorder raise in sheet_02 looks good.
- Worse: the cold blue-grey pillar hall from last build (prev_06) is gone. It had real colour contrast and mood, and it is now yellow like everything else (frame_17). I miss it.
- Worse: the glowing exit door that opened onto a cold blue room (prev_05) is gone. Frame_06 shows the same doorway opening onto yellow.
- Same: the monster, the sparse props and the lack of motion are unchanged. The silhouette in frame_01 is the same as last time.

## Steam review one-liner
"Beautiful hallways and a great camcorder, but everything is the same yellow and nothing is trying to scare me yet. Wishlisted, not bought."

## What I want next
- Give me rooms that aren't yellow and aren't evenly lit: a cold blue hall like last build, a nearly dark one, a green-tinted one. Let the colour change tell me I've gone somewhere worse.
- Make the thing in the distance matter. Show it closer, let me catch it in different places in the camcorder, and let a light flicker or die when it's near.
