# Player Review

## Gut reaction
It's a competent Backrooms. The yellow wallpaper, stained ceiling tiles and buzzing-light look is spot on, and a couple of frames look good. But everything I can see is empty yellow corridors. There's no camcorder, no chalk, no footprints, no monster, no wall outlined in duct tape and no seam to find. I don't see one thing in these stills that the store page promised. It runs at 39 fps on a 5070 Ti laptop with nothing in the scene. I'd wishlist it, not buy it yet.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] The game I'm looking at isn't the game on the store page. Across 21 frames and 4 sheets there is no camcorder, no chalk marks, no footprints, no dropped camera, no duct-tape wall and no monster. I wanted at least one frame where the pitch shows up, such as the viewfinder, a mark on a wall or something wrong in the distance. (There is also a video/clip.mp4 in the inbox that I can't view, so any of this could be in motion I didn't see.)
2. [ruins it] The frame rate is rough for what's on screen: 39 fps average and 27 fps lows at 1440p on a top-tier laptop GPU (perf.json). Hallways with a few tubes and bare walls should be much smoother. Stutter kills dread in a game built on slow walking.
3. [hurts] The dread is missing. The lighting is bright, even and safe in almost every frame (frame_01, frame_11, frame_13, frame_17, frame_19). The dark rooms (frame_05, frame_06) are the only moments with tension, and they look like unlit rooms, not menacing ones. I wanted light that fails, buzzes or leaves a corridor half-dark, and distance that gets murky and wrong.
4. [hurts] The shadows look glitchy. frame_04 has a jagged stair-stepped shadow edge on the left wall. frame_10 has stepped shadow bands on the floor, and frame_19 has a stair-stepped wet-carpet edge at right. Those read as rendering bugs, not mood. I wanted soft, believable shadow falloff.
5. [hurts] Everything is the same yellow. Walls, ceiling, carpet and light are all one yellow-brown, with no contrast in colour or surface (frame_15, frame_16, frame_19). Spaces blur together and nothing is memorable. I wanted at least one off-colour light, a stained or damaged area, or one thing that doesn't belong.
6. [hurts] No camera character. It looks like a clean modern render with soft edges. A found-footage camcorder game should show tape grain, timestamp, tracking wobble, flare off the white tubes and handheld sway. None of that is visible, and the ceiling tubes are blown-out white rectangles with no bloom.
7. [nitpick] Surfaces look too new. Baseboards, door frames (frame_21, frame_02) and wall corners are crisp and clean. Wallpaper tiles in straight vertical stripes with visible repetition, and the carpet has no wear, footprint or stain pattern beyond a repeating swirl.

## Working, don't touch
- The ceiling tile work: speckled panels, water-stain blotches (frame_09, frame_17), missing tiles showing black voids (frame_04) and the recessed odd panel (frame_08). Best surface detail in the build.
- The light pools on the walls under the tubes (frame_08, frame_10, sheet_03). Some soft falloff, which makes the rooms feel lit.
- Big pillared halls with long sightlines (frame_14, frame_08). That scale works.
- Doorways cut into partition walls (frame_21, sheet_01). A good, readable "where does that go" hook.
- The dark-room versus lit-room contrast around frame_04 and frame_05, as an idea. Keep it and fix the shadow edges.
