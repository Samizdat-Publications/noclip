# Player Review

## Gut reaction
The pillar hall is the first thing in this game I'd screenshot. Cold blue-grey fog, a lone folding chair, and a warm red EXIT glow far away (frame_17, frame_18, frame_19). The yellow office corridors around it are still the generic backrooms look, and the monster still reads as a glitchy 3D scan rather than something scary. It looks better than most backrooms clones. It's still a looks-first build, and I can't tell from stills that anything happens. I'd keep playing for the halls, not for the scares.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The monster doesn't scare me. In frame_11 the Occupant is a lumpy, see-through gold figure standing beside a pillar in good light. Its colour is the same as the wallpaper and carpet, so it reads as a texture glitch, not a presence. In frame_09 it is a gold claw in the bottom corner. I wanted a silhouette I'd mistake for a pillar until it moved, with a shape and edge that look lit by the same light as the room.
2. [hurts] The yellow rooms and corridors are the weakest part and take up most of the frames. The walls are uniform mustard, the ceiling grid is the same everywhere, and the carpet is flat. Frame_06 and frame_07 look like a template with a doorway. Next to the blue halls it looks like a different game. I wanted the warm areas to have at least one distinct, stranger place, such as an odd light colour, a ceiling that's wrong, or a wall that doesn't make sense.
3. [hurts] The corridor walls go to near-black when the camera is off-axis. Frame_02 has two huge dark slabs left and right, and frame_10 is almost entirely black. In sheet_02 six of nine frames are mostly dark. That reads as an unlit level, not menace. I wanted dark with a reason, like a dead panel at the end of a hall with the near wall still visible.
4. [hurts] Some frames have an odd green cast on the ceilings and orange tiles. See frame_19, frame_20 and frame_21 (top of frame), and the upper left of frame_16. It looks like a leftover colour bleed, not a deliberate dead-tube green. It lifts the fog and makes the halls look cheaper than they are. I'd either make it clearly sickly and intentional or remove it.
5. [nitpick] The columns are too clean. In frame_16 and frame_17 they are chalk-white, clean, evenly lit blocks, and the chipped tops all look identical. They are the main shape in the room, so they should look worn. The grime I can see is a faint smear near the base.
6. [nitpick] The camcorder look is inconsistent. Some frames have the overlay (frame_01, frame_03) and some don't (frame_18). The tape tear on the chalk door (frame_14) is great but is the only glitch I can see on a still. The blown-out door in frame_12 looks like an exposure effect, but nothing in the room tells me why it is there.
7. [nitpick] The static views are dull. In sheet_01 I'm looking at an empty room with a tripod for nine frames. Even a mild breathing sway, a flicker, or something changing in the room would help. The only camera movement I can judge is the hall walk in sheet_03, and it feels floaty and slow.

## Working, don't touch
- The blue fog halls with real column shadows and a warm red EXIT glow in the distance (frame_19, frame_20). This is the strongest image set and looks like a different tier from the yellow rooms.
- The change from warm to cold light in sheet_04, with the camera tearing as it goes.
- The floating high door on the tall wall (frame_09). This is a good wrong-space idea.
- The chalk door with "HERE" and its tape-tear version through the camcorder (frame_07 and frame_14). It tells the player what to do without text.
- The stained ceiling tiles and wallpaper chevrons up close (frame_13, frame_08).
- The lone folding chair in the middle of the hall (frame_18, sheet_03).
- Frame rate and stability.

## Compared to last build
Better, mostly in the halls. The old pillar hall (prev_05) was a yellow-beige fog with flat, same-lit columns and no shadow structure. Now it is blue fog with real shadows and a distant glow, and it's much stronger (frame_17 to frame_21). The corridors are about the same as before (prev_03 vs frame_08), with slightly better light spread along the hall. The old monster, a flat dark human silhouette (prev_01), has been swapped for the gold wallpaper figure. The old one read as a person but looked a bit like a cardboard cutout. The new one is less obviously fake in outline but less readable and not scary. I'd call that a sideways move. The chalk door (prev_02 vs frame_07) is almost the same, though the ceiling tile overhead is brighter and the lit panel at top right is nicer.

## Steam review one-liner
"The pillar halls are the best-looking backrooms I've seen since the real ones, but the monster is a gold glitch and I walked a lot of yellow corridors waiting for something to happen."

## What I want next
1. A monster I'd mistake for a pillar until it moved. It should be lit like the room, have a clear silhouette, and show up in the blue halls, where the dread is already working. Right now the best location has no threat in it.
2. Bring the quality of the blue halls to the yellow areas. Give the warm rooms their own weird light, one impossible thing per room like the high door, and walls dark only when there is a reason. I want a reason to leave the halls.
