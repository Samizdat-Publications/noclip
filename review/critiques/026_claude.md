# Player Review

## Gut reaction
Okay, so it's a very yellow Backrooms game with a camcorder, and the camcorder is the best idea in it. The tape view is where the game wakes up. The door you can only see on tape (frame_17) and the black rewind/playback screens (frame_18 to frame_21) are the first things I'd actually screenshot. The naked-eye world is clean but samey. Wallpaper, ceiling tiles and carpet repeat in every shot, and the whole picture is one flat mustard tone. The monster is a dark cutout that I can barely read, and I never felt hunted. This is a good-looking tech demo of a vibe, not yet a place that scares me.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] The monster doesn't scare me. In frame_03 and sheet_04 it's a dark paper-doll silhouette far away in a bright room, wobbling with streaks. In frame_15 it's a dim humanoid in a side room that I'd miss entirely. I want a shape that wrongly fills the frame, or something that sits too close and too still, not a distant cutout in an open room.
2. [hurts] The whole game is one mustard color. Frames 04, 08, 10, 12 and 13 could be swapped for each other. The only variety is brighter or darker yellow, and the fog makes it a little greener (frame_14). Give me one room with a different color of light, a failing tube, a pure black hole, anything. The previous build's cold blue-grey (prev_03, prev_05) is gone and nothing replaced that contrast.
3. [hurts] The naked-eye world is too evenly lit and too clean. Frame_16 and frame_08 show wide bright rooms with no hiding places and no real darkness. Dread comes from what I can't see, and I can see everywhere. The dark rooms (frame_15, frame_09) are just dimmer, not scarier.
4. [hurts] The carpet looks bad up close and from afar. In frame_05 and frame_16 it's an orange noise texture with blurry dark blotches. It sells neither wet nor dirty carpet, and it dominates the bottom third of most shots. The store page promises "carpet that never dries", and I don't see that.
5. [hurts] The surreal promise is thin. Nothing in these frames is impossible except the tape door. The "rooms nobody built" are ordinary partition rooms (frame_10, frame_13). I want a hall that is longer than the room it's in, a door that opens onto the wrong side, or wallpaper that continues across the ceiling.
6. [nitpick] The tape effects are flat in places. The glowing outline on the tape door (frame_17) reads like a debug highlight, and the white scribble streaks look cheap against the otherwise good grain. The playback-screen stripes are great, though.
7. [nitpick] Props feel sparse and generic. The ladder, milk crate and folding chair repeat (frame_07, frame_09, frame_10), and the dead worker in frame_07 is a tiny prop on a huge empty floor.

## Working, don't touch
- The VHS look, especially REW/PLAY/END OF TAPE (frames 18 to 21). That is your identity.
- The pillar hall silhouette and fog depth (frame_14, frame_16). They look good and the scale is right.
- Wallpaper chevron pattern, ceiling tiles with stains, and baseboards (frame_04, frame_12).
- The "door only the tape sees" idea and the chalk "HERE" door (frame_15 and frame_17).
- Pooled panel lighting in long halls (frame_04). Ceiling lights read as lights now.
- Performance headroom.

## Compared to last build
Better and worse. The new build is warmer and more coherent as a place: yellow wallpaper, carpet and tiles tie together, and the pillar bases look anchored (frame_16 vs prev_03). Last time's cold blue-grey pillar halls (prev_03, prev_05, prev_06) were moodier and had a clear focal point (the glowing exit door at the far end, the dark corridor in prev_02). That dramatic contrast is gone. Darkness in prev_02 was properly scary and this build doesn't have anything like it. The tape playback screens and the catalogue card are new and good. Overall I'd call it a sidegrade: more cohesive, less dramatic.

## Steam review one-liner
"The camcorder is a great hook and the VHS playback screens are gorgeous, but the monster is a smudge and every room is the same yellow. Wishlist, but I'm not scared yet."

## What I want next
1. Make the monster physically present: close, big and wrong-looking, in a dark enough room that I can't just spot it from 30 meters away.
2. Break the mustard. Give me one or two spaces with a different light and a really impossible layout, so the surreal pitch shows up in a screenshot.
