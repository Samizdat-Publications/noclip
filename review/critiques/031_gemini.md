# Player Review

## Gut reaction
I am usually exhausted by Backrooms clones, but the camcorder mechanic actually hooked me. Looking through the viewfinder to reveal a glowing chalk outline of a door that isn't there in reality is a fantastic, mind-bending hook. However, the game's actual environments look like a 2006 source engine map on fullbright. It has a great core idea, but the visual execution and performance are severely lacking.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] **The lighting is completely flat.** Look at the gray hallway in Still 02 and 03. There is no ambient occlusion where the walls meet the floor or ceiling. The fluorescent panels are just bright white textures that don't seem to cast directional light or have realistic falloff. It kills the horror vibe.
2. [ruins it] **Unacceptable performance.** Getting an average of 74 FPS and 1% lows of 39.9 FPS on a high-end RTX 5070 Ti Laptop for a game with basic geometry and repeating textures means something is fundamentally broken under the hood. 
3. [hurts] **Aggressive texture tiling.** The wallpaper in both levels (especially the chevron pattern in Still 02) repeats so obviously it looks like a placeholder. You need decals, dirt, water stains, or larger texture variations to make it look like a physical place.
4. [hurts] **Goofy monster ground animation.** The monster scrambling on the ceiling at 0:36 is a genuine 10/10 jump-scare moment. But when it charges you in Depth 2 (1:41) with its arms flailing out to the sides, it looks silly and loses all its menace.
5. [nitpick] **Flare light looks unnatural.** When the flare is thrown at 0:46, it just washes the entire area in a flat red tint. It doesn't cast dynamic shadows from the dead body or highlight any normal maps on the carpet.
6. [nitpick] **Razor-sharp shadows.** The shadow cast by the yellow gate in Still 04 has perfectly crisp, hard edges. It looks like it's being cast by a point light in a game engine rather than ambient fluorescent tubes, which would cast very soft, diffused shadows.

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder's hidden reality mechanic. Seeing the word "HERE" and the outline of a door (Still 01) is brilliant.
- The monster's ceiling-crawling behavior. Do more of that.
- The audio design. The heavy, distorted screech when the monster spots you triggers instant panic.

## Steam review one-liner
A brilliant camcorder mechanic and terrifying sound design trapped inside a visually flat, unoptimized Backrooms clone.

## What I want next
A massive lighting and shadow overhaul to make the environments actually look dark and scary, and a fix for whatever is tanking the framerate.
