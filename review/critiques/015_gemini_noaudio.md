# Player Review

## Gut reaction
I love the core concept of using a camcorder to reveal hidden geometry and messages, and the glitchy transition through the chalk door is genuinely sick. However, the environment art is completely working against the atmosphere right now. The lighting is incredibly flat, the materials look like matte cardboard, and the lack of shadows makes everything feel like an unlit blockout rather than a real, terrifying place. It has the bones of a great analog horror game, but it desperately needs a visual overhaul to sell the dread.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] **Zero ambient occlusion or contact shadows.** In Frame 3 and Video 01:14, the black office partitions cast literally no shadow on the floor. The walls meet the ceiling and floor with perfect, harsh lines. It makes the environment look like a 2004 game without baked lighting.
2. [hurts] **Flat, unreactive materials.** The store page promises "carpet that never dries," but the floor is a completely matte, dry-looking tan texture (Frame 2). It needs roughness maps, specular highlights, and subtle puddle decals to actually look damp and gross.
3. [hurts] **Lighting lacks source and direction.** In Frame 3, there's a bright bloom spot on the ceiling, but no light fixture there. The fluorescent panels in the hallways are glowing rectangles, but they don't cast distinct, harsh cones of light or shadows down the corridor.
4. [nitpick] **Jarring light transitions.** At Video 00:18, the transition from the lit hallway into the dark zone is incredibly abrupt. It looks like a broken fog setting or a missing light probe rather than a natural, gradual falloff of light into darkness.

## Working, don't touch
- The camcorder reveal mechanic. Turning a 2D chalk outline into a 3D, glitching tear in reality (Frame 4, Video 00:26) is an awesome visual trick.
- The subtle surrealism, like the door placed 15 feet up the wall (Frame 3) and the towering, distorted shadow of the tripod (Video 00:30).
- The VHS overlay. It's clean and readable without relying on obnoxious amounts of chromatic aberration or screen tearing.

## Steam review one-liner
A fantastic camcorder mechanic trapped inside a Backrooms map that forgot to bake its lighting.

## What I want next
A complete lighting and material pass. Give me damp, reflective carpets, dark corners with heavy ambient occlusion, and fluorescent lights that actually cast harsh contact shadows.
