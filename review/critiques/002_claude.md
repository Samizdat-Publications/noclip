# Player Review

## Gut reaction
This is a good-looking Backrooms corridor sim that doesn't yet feel like the game on the store page. The ceiling tiles, wallpaper and fluorescent look are well observed, and the camcorder view is a strong hook. But across 21 stills and 4 sheets I see no monster, no chalk marks, no tape, no seams, and no impossible space. It's yellow hallway after yellow hallway. I'd wishlist it and wait. It also runs worse than empty rooms should on a high-end laptop GPU.

I couldn't watch `review/inbox/video/clip.mp4`, so none of this covers video.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] Nothing happens. The store page promises a monster, chalk marks, footprints, and "the hallway behind you was shorter." Only footprints show up (frame_16, frame_20). I never see the creature, the duct-tape wall, chalk, or the dropped camera. Show me one thing that's wrong in the frame.
2. [hurts] Every space looks the same. Frames 05, 06, 12, 17, 21 and sheets 01 and 03 are interchangeable yellow corridors. I want a visual landmark every few rooms: a different light color, a flooded stretch, a dead light zone, something furniture-store-ish.
3. [hurts] Performance is poor for what's on screen. 39.6 fps lows at 1440p on a top laptop GPU means mid-range PCs will struggle with simple hallways.
4. [hurts] The ceiling lights look like stickers. Each panel is a flat white rectangle with little bloom (frame_08, frame_14, frame_19). The wall-wash near the lights is nice, so carry that glow into the air and onto the carpet below them.
5. [hurts] The dark rooms are murky brown with no gradient (frame_10, frame_11, frame_19, sheet_02). They look like a lighting cutoff, not the absence of light. I want a dim falloff with faint spill and shapes at the edge.
6. [nitpick] The jagged stair-stepped edge on the right wall in frame_10 (and sheet_02) looks like a rendering glitch.
7. [nitpick] The camcorder overlay never changes. The REC dot only blinks, the battery stays at 3/4, and the timestamp just ticks (sheet_03). The camera could degrade, glitch or flare when something is near. The hard cut between camcorder view and clean view (sheet_04) needs a transition.

## Working, don't touch
- The red EXIT sign glow on the ceiling and walls (frame_04). It's the best lighting beat in the build.
- The camcorder look (frame_01, frame_09, frame_18): grain, vignette, timestamp and scanline tear.
- The water stains, tide line and drip streaks on the wall (frame_08), plus the ceiling stains.
- The footprints on carpet (frame_16, frame_20).
- The pillar-hall scale (frame_14).
- The shadow wedges from doorways in frame_18 and frame_19. The dark-to-lit read is strong.

## Compared to last build
Better. The old stills (prev_01 to prev_06) were clean but sterile: glossy rubbery floor, no stains, no props, flat walls. This build adds water-damaged walls and floor, footprints, an exit sign, doors, ceiling stains, and the camcorder view. The floor is now matte carpet instead of plastic. The environment is more convincing and has more story. The dark zones are still murky, and the jagged shadow edge from prev_04 is still there in frame_10. The bigger gap is still missing gameplay content.

## Steam review one-liner
Gorgeous beige hallways with great found-footage framing, but I walked for ten minutes and nothing looked back at me. Wishlisted.

## What I want next
- One real wrongness in the space: a hallway that visibly changes when I look away, or my own chalk marks in the wrong place. The store page promises it.
- Show me the monster, even once, only through the camcorder lens, with the image breaking up when it's close.
