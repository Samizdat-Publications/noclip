# Player Review

## Gut reaction
It looks like the Backrooms done competently, and the camcorder view is what sells it. Through the VHS filter the hallways feel like found footage. With the filter off (frame_04, frame_10, frame_20), the same hallway is flat mustard wallpaper in a tube. The tripod pocket (frame_19) and the glowing tape-seam door (frame_15) are the only two images I'd screenshot. Everything else is the same corridor again. I'd keep playing for the weird rooms, but I'm not yet scared, and nothing here says it's more than a good liminal-space tech demo.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] Almost every frame is the same hallway: wallpaper, tiles, carpet, a light every few metres (frame_10, frame_12, frame_18, frame_20, frame_21, sheet_03). I stop being disoriented and start being bored. I wanted something in the geometry to tell zones apart, like turns, a dead end, a visible landmark or a change of scale.
2. [hurts] The unfiltered view is flat and drab (frame_04, frame_10, frame_20). Walls are huge unbroken planes of the same yellow with a faint stripe, and the corridor has no focal point. The camcorder filter does the real work. Without it I notice the walls don't have enough variation.
3. [hurts] The monster barely registers. In the one clear shot (frame_01, sheet_04) it is a black cutout standing still at the far end, with no light on it and no sense of mass. I need to see what it does, such as a limb out of place or movement that changes between sheet frames. As shown, it's a mannequin.
4. [hurts] The camcorder in hand (frame_18) is a dark, flat-shaded box with a lens. It reads as a pistol in the lower right, and its surface has no highlights or wear. It's the one object I look at all game, so it should look like a real, scuffed 90s camcorder.
5. [hurts] The tall scale-error room (frame_17) is a clever idea but looks unfinished. There are two huge blank walls, a tiny ceiling light, and a trim strip with a gap in it (around x=465-675) that looks like a modelling error rather than something eerie. Coloured fringing runs down both screen edges. Give it scale cues like a door that is too small or a light far above.
6. [nitpick] The carpet shows horizontal smeared bands in the middle distance (frame_04 around y=780, frame_12, frame_20). They read as a blurry artifact, not a wet reflection. The dark wet patches are fine, but these streaks look like a rendering smudge.
7. [nitpick] frame_06 is a full black frame. If it's a blackout or a death beat, fine. If it's a hitch or a failed transition, it will read as a bug.

- The tape-seam doorway seen through the camera, with the chalk drawing and the tripod blown out in white (frame_15). It's the best "the camera sees things you can't" moment.
- The tripod room: orange survey light, hard tripod shadow, stencil "G1 S-14", crate and floppy disk (frame_19). This is the visual benchmark for the rest of the game.
- The environmental storytelling props: the "COPY 31" tally marks (frame_18), the lone folding chair (frame_13) and the "LOOK THROUGH IT" chalk drawing (frame_03).

## Compared to last build
Better, but only in places.
- Door trim went from pale grey with a black void (prev_03) to dark wood (frame_03, frame_09). It sits better in the palette.
- The camcorder went from a huge grey faceted blob (prev_06) to a smaller black body (frame_18), though it still looks flat.
- The tape-seam doorway is more controlled. The blown-out white rectangle (prev_02) became a glowing door with the tripod and chalk readable inside (frame_15).
- There is more set dressing: the tripod room, the tally marks, wall stains and ceiling stains.
- Carpet looks more photographic (compare prev_03 with frame_11).
- The earlier open-plan rooms with pillars (prev_01, prev_05) are barely represented in this footage. This build is mostly corridors and has lost some spatial variety.

## Steam review one-liner
Great found-footage camcorder, nice props, but I've walked down the same yellow hallway ten times and nothing has jumped out yet. Wishlisted.

## What I want next
- Make each stretch of corridor look different, with new materials, light colours and layouts, and put the strange rooms (the tripod pocket, the tall room, the chalk door) closer together. That way the game is mostly its best frames (frame_19, frame_15).
- Show me the monster in motion and in light. Let it break the geometry or do something wrong in a lit corridor, so I'd want to film it.
