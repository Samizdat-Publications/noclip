# Player Review

## Gut reaction
I am so incredibly burnt out on Backrooms-style yellow wallpaper games, but the audio and core mechanic here actually hooked me. The tension of hearing a monster sprinting at you while seeing absolutely nothing in normal vision is fantastic, and needing to pull out the camcorder to spot it creates a great risk/reward loop. That said, visually, this looks like a decade-old asset flip. The lighting is completely broken in places and the performance is shockingly bad for how simple the geometry is.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [ruins it] **No shadow casting.** Look at the second standalone image with the tripod. There is a bright spotlight hitting the wall directly behind it, and the tripod casts absolutely zero shadow. It makes the object look photoshopped into the scene. Turn on dynamic shadows.
2. [hurts] **Sticker-like ceiling lights.** In the third image (the pillar room) and the starting hallway, the fluorescent ceiling lights are just pure white rectangles. They have no bloom, they don't bounce light into the surrounding ceiling tiles, and they don't scatter in the fog. 
3. [hurts] **Horrendous optimization.** You are getting a 78 FPS average and 44 FPS 1% lows on a high-end 50-series laptop GPU. For a game with boxy rooms, flat textures, and basic depth fog, this is wildly unoptimized. 
4. [nitpick] **Monster design contradicts the pitch.** The store page says the monster is "made of the same paper as the walls," but on the camcorder (first image and 1:37 in video), it looks like a blue/black static void silhouette. Make it actually look like torn, moving wallpaper if that's the lore.
5. [nitpick] **Cheap VHS snow.** The white static dots during the rewind playback (fourth image) look like a cheap 2D particle overlay rather than actual magnetic tape degradation. 

## Working, don't touch
- The sound design is perfect. Do not change the wet carpet footsteps or the horrific, distorted breathing/skittering of the monster.
- The "shadow with no caster" trick in the green room. That is incredible, subtle surrealism.
- The core mechanic of the monster being completely invisible to the naked eye but audible. 
- The VHS rewind effect when you die.

## Steam review one-liner
Looks like a 2012 tech demo, sounds like an absolute nightmare—the camcorder mechanic saves this from being just another Backrooms clone.

## What I want next
Fix the glaring lighting and shadow omissions so the game stops looking like an asset flip. I also want to see more surreal, impossible-geometry tricks like the disconnected shadow to really push the "rooms nobody built" vibe.
