# Player Review

## Gut reaction
The tripod rooms are what I'll remember from this build: a camcorder on a stand, its huge black shadow climbing the orange wall, "DO NOT REMOVE" tape on the side. That's real analog-horror stuff and I'd screenshot it. The rest of the 40 seconds is empty yellow boxes, a few rooms so dark I couldn't see anything, and door frames that look like melted chocolate. Nothing hunted me, nothing showed up at the end of a hall, and nothing happened. Great camcorder vibe, but the place feels empty instead of wrong.

## Top problems (ranked, max 7)
1. [hurts] **The dark rooms crush to pure black.** sheet_02 is nine panels (about 4 seconds) of black screen with a few glowing door rectangles. frame_08 is brown mud where you can't read the floor, walls or the pillar. Dark should be scary, not unreadable. I wanted faint edges, one dying tube, a sliver of light on the carpet, something to steer by and then imagine things in.
2. [hurts] **Nothing scary appears.** No figure in 21 frames and 4 sheets. The last build had a silhouette standing at the end of a hallway (prev_01), and that was the scariest image in either build. The store page promises "rare scares", but a 40-second capture should still have one moment where I tense up.
3. [hurts] **Door trim looks melted.** In close-ups (frame_01, frame_09, frame_18) the casing is a wobbly, bubbly brown texture like wet bark or chocolate. In frame_08 a whole pillar is wrapped in it, and so is the left pillar in frame_06. Next to the believable wallpaper and carpet it's the first thing that screams "game asset". I wanted flat painted or veneer trim with crisp edges and some chipped paint.
4. [hurts] **The tall rooms read as greybox.** frame_04, frame_05 and frame_13 are giant blank yellow boxes with one soft wash of light and a tiny door. Nothing tells me how big the room is, so it doesn't feel impossible, just empty. I wanted something that shows the scale: drop-ceiling grid way up, tubes stacked high, the top fading into darkness, or a second doorway halfway up the wall. The small light at the top of frame_13 is the right idea; push it.
5. [hurts] **The green room is radioactive.** sheet_01 and the back room in frame_01 are saturated lime green, like a color filter. Old fluorescents go sickly pale green-grey, not highlighter.
6. [nitpick] **The seam doorway glows like a sci-fi forcefield.** In frame_03 and sheet_03 it has a crackling white outline, more like a selection highlight than tape damage. The chalk outline in plain view (frame_12) is much creepier and subtler. The camera view should look like the tape is struggling, not like a portal.
7. [nitpick] **The camcorder in your hand is a toy, and the tripod shadow has jagged edges.** The camcorder fills a third of the screen in sheet_03 panel 1 and in frame_04, with a flat-shaded lens and chunky plastic. The tripod shadow in frame_20 and frame_21 has stair-stepped edges on the right leg that don't look like tape blur.

## Working, don't touch
- **The survey tripod rooms** (frame_10, frame_20, frame_21): hard work light, oversized shadow on the wall, red stencil "D G1 S-14" with an arrow, milk crate, tape cassette on the floor, "HD-4 REC / DO NOT REMOVE" label, water damage running down from the ceiling. Best content in the build.
- **Carpet with footprints and damp patches** (frame_06, frame_07, frame_19, sheet_01). Footprints you didn't make are quietly creepy.
- **The camcorder overlay** (frame_02, frame_11, frame_17): scanlines, noise strip at the bottom, timestamp, blinking REC, battery icon. The full-frame tracking tear when you raise the camera (sheet_01, last panel) is a great transition.
- **Lit room seen from a dark room** (frame_06, frame_15, frame_16). Those frames have real depth.
- **Long foggy corridors** (frame_14), and the ceiling tiles with stains and dead-bug specks in the light panels.
- **Chalk "HERE" door outline in plain view** (frame_12).
- **Frame rate.** Keep it at this level.

## Compared to last build
- **Better:** more room variety. Lighting moods (orange tripod room, green room, dark rooms), the survey props and stencils, footprints on the carpet, and the seam you can only see through the camcorder (sheet_03 showing the tripod room behind the chalk door) are all new and mostly good.
- **Worse:** the figure is gone. prev_01's hallway silhouette was the strongest scare across both builds, and this capture has no equivalent. The new dark rooms overshoot into black (sheet_02, frame_08). I can't tell whether the lumpy door trim is new, because the old stills don't show it this close, but in frame_01, frame_09 and frame_18 it looks bad.
- **Same:** the corridor look (frame_14 vs prev_02 and prev_04), wallpaper, ceiling grid and fog falloff. The "LOOK THROUGH IT" graffiti from prev_02 doesn't appear in this capture; the chalk "HERE" door plays a similar role.

## Steam review one-liner
"The camcorder rooms with the tripods are legit creepy and the VHS filter is spot on, but most of the time you're staring at an empty yellow box or a black screen waiting for something to happen."

## What I want next
1. Put something in the capture. A figure at the end of a hall, a tripod that's turned to face me when I come back, a chair that moved. One real "wait, what" moment in the first minute.
2. Make the tall rooms and dark rooms readable and awe-inspiring instead of blank or black, and fix the melted door trim, because it shows up in nearly every doorway shot.
